High-Visibility Tape & Safety Apparel

Engineered for maximum low-light luminance, retroreflective performance, and strict adherence to Canadian Safety Association (CSA) standards.

High-Visibility Tape Overview

High-visibility tape can be customized in terms of the size, colour and placement. We always recommend a Canadian Safety Association (CSA) placement when possible to ensure that standards are met.

The CSA Z96-15 addresses high visibility safety apparel. There are various options and classifications for garments that feature reflective tape. We can work together to find the best solution for your uniforms.

CSA Z96-15 Garment Classes & Trim Levels

As per the CSA Z96-15 standard, there are three primary classes of garments and three designated trim levels (Class 1, Class 2, Class 3).

Class 1 Apparel

Designed for lower-risk environments where traffic does not exceed 40 km/h and workers have full separation from oncoming traffic.

Class 2 Apparel

Intermediate protection for roadway construction, parking attendants, and environments with traffic speeds up to 80 km/h.

Class 3 Apparel

Maximum visibility for high-speed roadways, emergency response personnel, and hazardous low-light work zones.

Alternative Classes

Specialized configurations and alternative garment groupings engineered to accommodate unique industry hazard profiles.

Daytime vs. Nighttime Visibility

Reflective tape is categorized as either Daytime Visible or Nighttime Visible:

  • Daytime Visible: Includes tape that is bright yellow/green and/or bright orange for high daylight luminance.
  • Nighttime Visible: Refers to Retroreflective Material—silver tape that bounces light back directly toward the light source for excellent low-light visibility.

Nighttime Visibility Tape Options:

  • 2” Silver – Solid
  • 2” Silver Segmented
  • 2” Combined Performance (Yellow – Silver, Orange - Silver) *allowable for FR applications
  • 4” Combined Performance (Yellow – Silver, Orange – Silver)

Flame Resistant (FR) High-Visibility Options

Of course, there are also options that meet the above specifications but are also Flame Resistant (FR) should you require a garment to be both FR and High Visibility.

Special design and performance allowances apply for garments intended for Flame Resistant (FR) applications to ensure arc-flash and thermal protection ratings remain uncompromised.
