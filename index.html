01. About Me

I am a Cybersecurity Researcher and IT professional currently working in Sales Operations IT Support. My technical foundation is uniquely hands-on: beginning as a licensed automotive mechanic, I developed a deeply analytical, root-cause approach to troubleshooting complex physical systems that I now apply to IT infrastructure.

My expertise bridges the physical and digital domains. I'm well-versed in embedded hardware modifications, micro-soldering, and retrofitting legacy electronics. On the digital front, I actively develop hands-on experience through TryHackMe, focusing on Windows and Linux digital forensics, static malware analysis, and system architecture. Holding an ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity credential and a certificate in cybersecurity from St. Francis Xavier University, I leverage both my hardware intuition and security training to engineer robust, resilient systems.
